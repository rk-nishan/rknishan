About Me

Microbiologist integrating molecular diagnostics, microbial genomics, cancer multi-omics, and computational vaccine design.

I am an MPhil researcher in Microbiology and Research Assistant at the Advanced Molecular Biology Laboratory, Jagannath University, Dhaka. My current work develops a low-cost strip-based DNA hybridization assay for detecting selected Gram-positive nosocomial pathogens together with their resistance and virulence markers.

My research combines microbial culture, antimicrobial susceptibility testing, PCR, probe design, membrane hybridization, and analytical validation with computational workflows in R and Python. I also work with RNA-seq, ceRNA networks, survival analysis, mutation and methylation data, immune infiltration, drug-sensitivity prediction, single-cell RNA-seq, and whole-genome sequence analysis.

Across more than three years of research experience, I have contributed to a peer-reviewed PLOS ONE article, submitted an ESBL-producing Escherichia coli genome dataset to NCBI, and presented microbiology and lung-cancer research at national conferences. My long-term goal is to connect practical laboratory diagnostics with reproducible bioinformatics to address clinically important infectious diseases and cancer.
